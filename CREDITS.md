## Recorded audio (`public/audio/`)

Clips are produced by `scripts/audio/generate.py`. Which licence applies
depends on the provider used to make them:

- **Azure neural voices** (`af-ZA-AdriNeural`, `af-ZA-WillemNeural`):
  generated audio may be used commercially under the Azure AI Speech terms.
  No attribution required.
- **Mimic 3 `af_ZA/google-nwu_low`** (run locally via sherpa-onnx): the voice
  is licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0** and was trained on the Google / North-West
  University corpus "High quality TTS data for Afrikaans"
  (https://www.openslr.org/32/, CC BY-SA 4.0). Commercial use is allowed, but
  you must credit the source (this file plus a line in the app's about/settings
  screen is enough) and the clips themselves stay under CC BY-SA.

Voices deliberately NOT used because their licences forbid commercial use:
Meta MMS-TTS (`facebook/mms-tts-afr`, CC BY-NC 4.0) and the unofficial
Microsoft Edge "read aloud" endpoint (`edge-tts`).

## Course content

Vocabulary, sentences, grammar notes, dialogues and memory hooks were written
for this app. They were drafted with AI assistance and have NOT yet been
proofread by a native Afrikaans speaker — do that before charging for it.
